What this lab does — and does not do

Extracted; not indexed. After parsing, build source-linked chunks. Chunked; not embedded. Explicitly download the pinned encoder to continue. Embedded; not retrieved. Run an explicit question to obtain ranked candidates. Context; not generated. Assemble all selected evidence with exact quotes and source mappings. Candidate scores do not establish financial facts, table columns, reading order or OCR.

Text is the original parser-extracted string, not a raw PDF byte offset. PDF.js can replace whitespace. Geometry describes a text item, not every character.

Replacing a document first revokes the old operation's right to commit and clears its dependent artifacts. Cancelling chunk work preserves the accepted source; cancelling embedding preserves source and chunks; cancelling a query preserves source, chunks and passage vectors. No partial output is committed.
