Accepted embedding receipt

Verified, fetched and cached counts describe asset body bytes, not all network traffic. Attempt elapsed includes loading, initialization, encoding and disposal; it is not isolated GPU time. A WebGPU provider does not prove every operator ran on the GPU; mean pooling runs in JavaScript on the CPU.

What this lab does — and does not do

Extracted; not indexed. After parsing, build source-linked chunks. Chunked; not embedded. Explicitly download the pinned encoder to continue. Embedded; not retrieved. Accepted output is a representation, with no retrieval or generation yet. It does not establish financial facts, table columns, reading order or OCR.

Text is the original parser-extracted string, not a raw PDF byte offset. PDF.js can replace whitespace. Geometry describes a text item, not every character.

Replacing a document first revokes the old operation's right to commit and clears its dependent artifacts. Cancelling chunk work preserves the accepted source; cancelling embedding preserves source and chunks. Neither commits partial output.
