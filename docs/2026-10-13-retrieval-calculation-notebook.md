# Inspect the arithmetic behind a retrieved passage

The browser notebook now connects its accepted query and passage vectors to the displayed ranking. After a successful query, open **Notebook**, then **Inspect how similarity selects passages**. The disclosure compares all retained chunks, names the selected Top K, and offers a complete coordinate calculation for any retained passage.

| Before | After | Why |

The computation is an explicitly labeled **post-acceptance inspection recomputation**. Production source/chunk copiers preserve page, parser-item and source-span identity. Query/passage copiers validate representation and receipts. The same exact ranker recomputes the full retained population, and the view checks that its selected prefix, query norm, dot products, passage norms and cosines exactly agree with the accepted retrieval. It never rewrites the accepted result or invokes an encoder.

For a selected source ordinal, the component record multiplies corresponding coordinates in increasing order, retains each product and cumulative dot, separately accumulates query/passage sums of squares, and computes their square-root norms and product. The final division reproduces the accepted scalar. Near-unit accepted vectors still use their measured norms, following the [normalized-dot-product definition](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.metrics.pairwise.cosine_similarity.html). No individual learned coordinate receives a word, topic or financial interpretation. Browser MiniLM scores do not inherit the Python BGE threshold.

The comparison and calculation exports use the shared complete-record disclosure: exact UTF-8 JSON, no added newline, consecutive bounded text pages and explicit download. Full source IDs and unrounded values are retained even where the overview uses concise labels. Presentation owners are separate from accepted artifacts; window changes, selection changes, closing, rerender, question/Top K edits, active work, replacement, Clear and pagehide retire the applicable saved controls and download URLs. Returning to an inspectable idle state mounts a fresh owner over retained artifacts.
